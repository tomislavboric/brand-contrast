Any brand colour, readable everywhere.

A customer's brand colour is arbitrary. A near-white yellow and a near-black navy are both legal brand colours, and they need opposite treatment. Pick one below and see the text, link and button-label colours derived from it. Every pair is checked against WCAG 2.1.
